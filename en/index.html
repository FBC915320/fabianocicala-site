About

Experience built by doing.

My journey has been built across advertising, publishing, graphics, industry, commerce, product development, markets, technology and entrepreneurship. I am the founder of CIKALA and have spent over three decades creating products, opening markets, developing people, facing crises, making decisions, failing, succeeding and learning. Today part of this experience also becomes books, content and conversations about leadership, entrepreneurship, innovation, artificial intelligence, faith and purpose.

Profile

I am a professional with more than 30 years of experience creating, developing and managing commercial operations and processes, with a career across different companies and as founder of CIKALA. My work combines strategy, B2B/B2C sales, new markets, product development, negotiation, international sourcing, importing, e-commerce, technology and management.
